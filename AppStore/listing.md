## Description (4000 max)
Pastry remembers everything you copy, so you never lose a link, a snippet, or a screenshot again.

Press ⇧⌘V in any app and Pastry shows your clipboard history right next to your cursor. Type to search, use the arrow keys to pick, and press Return: Pastry pastes it straight into the app you were using.

EVERYTHING YOU COPY
• Text, links, images, screenshots, and files from Finder
• Formatting is kept: bold, links, and fonts come back when you paste
• Press ⇧Return to paste as plain text instead

FAST
• Search your whole history as you type
• ⌘1 to ⌘9 paste the first nine items instantly
• Pin the things you use all the time so they stay at the top

YOURS
• Your history stays on your Mac. Pastry has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking, and it makes no network connections
• Copies that password managers mark as private are never recorded
• Pause recording any time from the menu bar

FITS IN
• Lives quietly in your menu bar, with no Dock icon
• Choose your shortcut, or record your own
• Keep 50 to 1,000 items, plus anything you pin
• Starts when you log in, if you like

To paste for you, Pastry asks for Accessibility access. It uses it only to press ⌘V when you choose an item. Without it, Pastry copies your choice and you press ⌘V yourself.

Pastry is free and open source: github.com/H3XY/Pastry
